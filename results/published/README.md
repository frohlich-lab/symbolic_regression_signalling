# Published results

Result artefacts underlying the manuscript

> Symbolic regression enables coarse-grained model discovery of intracellular
> signalling dynamics. T. de Pomereu, F. Fröhlich.

These are the hyperparameter grids and per-context metrics referred to in the
paper's Data, Materials, and Software Availability statement. Everything here is
regenerated by the Snakemake workflow in this repository; the copies are kept
under version control so that the numbers reported in the paper can be checked
without re-running the pipeline.

The full `data/` tree (≈20 GB) is not version controlled. The synthetic
enzyme-kinetics datasets are regenerated deterministically by the workflow from
the seeds recorded in `config.yaml`.

## Provenance

Generated by the pipeline in this repository under the configurations described
in the SI Appendix. Analyses used fixed seed 42 unless stated; degradation and
experimental runs used seeds 42–44.

The experimental measurements analysed here are not redistributed. They are the
mass cytometry data of Lun et al., available from Mendeley Data at
https://doi.org/10.17632/3kh7ypz232.1 (CC BY 4.0).
